Features

Everything HR does every day, in one place

Attendance

Clock in right on your phone

Employees open the app and tap Check-in. BossKin verifies location and device before recording.

  • Clock-in only works within a radius around the office, set per company or per department
  • Each account is tied to exactly one phone — no buddy punching
  • Rejects spoofed locations and very weak GPS signals
Shifts

Schedule a whole factory in minutes

Shift roster by pay period, scroll through the whole month, import and export with Excel.

  • Fixed shifts, overnight shifts, split shifts and flexible shifts that only need the required hours
  • Rotation templates generate schedules for many people at once
  • Employees request to swap or hand over shifts, managers approve
  • Work calendars per department (half-day Saturdays…) and yearly holidays, for correct standard working days
Leave

Request leave in the app, approve with one tap

Leave and time-correction requests are sent from the phone and go straight to the approver.

  • The leave ledger deducts only days that have a shift, and refunds when a request is cancelled
  • Leave by the day or by the hour, automatically excluding lunch break
  • Leave accrues monthly according to your company's rules
Period close

Close the period with one button

Approve all attendance for the company or a department, then lock the period so figures can't be changed.

  • Approve a whole period for 300 employees in under a minute
  • Locking a period blocks edits and new requests into the closed period
  • Late arrival, early leave, and day and night overtime are calculated automatically
Payroll

Payroll calculates itself, payslips on the phone

Pay is calculated from closed work days, paid leave and your company's own formulas.

  • Personal income tax by the 2026 schedule, with personal and dependent deductions; insurance with contribution caps
  • Pay and allowances come from the contract; mid-period raise addenda are still calculated correctly
  • Pay grades by day or by hour, with your own variables and formulas
  • Accountants cannot approve or pay out their own salary
Reports & security

Clear figures, tight permissions

Everyone sees and does only their own part of the work.

  • 8 Excel reports: HR, attendance, overtime, payroll cost, yearly overview
  • Permissions by role and by department
  • An audit log records every sensitive action; two-factor authentication for administrators
Employee records

Complete employee records, no extra software

Employment contracts

Probation, fixed-term and indefinite contracts, addenda, allowances. Reminders before a contract or probation ends.

Career history

Transfers, promotions and pay grade changes with effective dates; an org chart based on direct managers.

Tax & insurance

Tax code, social insurance book, and dependents deducted by month; sensitive numbers are masked for people without permission.

HR documents

ID cards, degrees and signed contracts are stored privately and opened only through temporary links; alerts for documents that are expiring or missing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is BossKin priced?

Pricing is by number of employees and the features you turn on, from just 5,000 VND per employee per month. You get a 1-month free trial with real data, help with setup and 24/7 incident support, then receive a quote matched to your size and the features you need.

Do I need to buy a time clock?

No. Employees clock in with their own phones; BossKin checks the GPS location and only accepts the device registered to the account.

What if an employee changes phones?

An administrator unbinds the device for that employee; the next clock-in on the new phone is bound again. The action is recorded in the audit log.

Does payroll include insurance and tax?

Yes. Payroll calculates social insurance 8%, health insurance 1.5%, unemployment insurance 1% and progressive personal income tax. You can define your own pay formulas and variables.

Are overnight, split and flexible shifts supported?

Yes. Up to 4 shifts per day; overnight shifts count night hours correctly; flexible shifts only require the full number of hours within the allowed window.

What devices does it run on?

An Android app for employees and a web version for managers, HR and accountants on a computer. The interface is available in Vietnamese and English.

Can I move my existing Excel data over?

Yes. Employees, departments, shifts and rosters can be imported from Excel; every import shows a preview of the changes before saving.
