## View a payslip

1. Open **My payslips** (*My space* group). Change the year with the arrows at the top right.
2. Each row is a month: workdays and net salary. Click a month to see the details.

![List of payslips by month](img:self-payroll)

The detailed payslip includes:

- **Workdays / hours**: actual days worked over standard days, day / night shift hours, overtime hours.
- **Gross salary**: pay by attendance + allowances + bonuses.
- **Deductions**: insurance (social insurance (BHXH) 8%, health insurance (BHYT) 1.5%, unemployment insurance (BHTN) 1% on the insured salary, with a cap), personal income tax (PIT), and other deductions.
- **Net salary** = gross salary − total deductions.
- Last line: **insured salary for BHXH/BHYT** and **family deduction** (self + dependents) used to calculate tax.

![Payslip details](img:payslip)

You only see payslips that accounting has **approved**. If you see wrong figures, tell accounting before payday.
