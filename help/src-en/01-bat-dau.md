## Sign in

BossKin runs in a web browser (computer) and as an Android phone app. Your company gives you the sign-in address.

1. Enter the work **Email** that HR registered for you.
2. Enter your **Password**. Click the eye icon to see what you typed.
3. Click **Sign in**. Leave **Remember me** unchecked on a shared computer — you will have to sign in again after closing the browser.

![Sign-in screen: email, password, Sign in button](img:login)

> **Note:** After several wrong passwords in a row, the account is locked for a few minutes to prevent password guessing.

## Change your password on first sign-in

Accounts created by HR (added manually, imported from Excel or via "Export temporary passwords") have a **temporary password**. On your first sign-in, BossKin
takes you straight to the change-password screen; you cannot use anything else until you change it.

1. Enter your **Current password** (the temporary one you were given).
2. Enter the **New password** twice: at least 8 characters, with both letters and digits.
3. Click **Change password**. The system signs out your old sessions and you sign in again with the new password.

## Home screen and menu

The menu on the left is split into groups. You **only see the items you have permission for** — regular employees see fewer items than managers or HR.

| Group | Contains |
|---|---|
| Overview | Today's figures (for managers) |
| My space | Check-in / Check-out, My payslips, Personal info |
| Requests & approvals | Attendance requests, Leave, Shift swaps, Team approvals, Edit requests |
| People | Employees, Org chart, Departments, Labor contracts, HR documents |
| Shifts & attendance | Shifts, Attendance |
| Payroll & reports | Payroll, Reports, Import / Export |
| Settings & system | Company settings, Roles, Audit log |

![Employee's screen: the menu has only their own items](img:home-employee)

![Administrator's screen: all groups](img:home-admin)

Click a **group title** to collapse / expand it; BossKin remembers your choice. The group containing the screen you are on is always open.
The ☰ button at the top left hides / shows the whole menu to give tables more room.

![Collapsing the "People" group](img:menu-collapse)

On a phone, the menu is behind the ☰ button, and a bottom navigation bar holds the most-used items.

## Account, language and sign-out

Click your **avatar** (the first letter of your name) at the top right:

1. **Personal info** — profile, change password, two-factor authentication, light / dark theme.
2. **Language** — switch between Tiếng Việt / English. All text and error messages change accordingly.
3. **Logout**.

## Notifications

The bell icon shows how many notifications are unread: requests approved / rejected, attendance reminders, contracts about to expire… Click it for a quick look;
open the **Notifications** menu (*My space* group) to see everything, mark as read or delete.

- **The inbox follows your account**: a notification sent to phone A is still there when you sign in on device B (another phone or a computer).
  Settings are saved per account too. Push alerts (shown outside the app) go to one device only — the one where you turned them on last.
- **Settings** tab: **Shift reminder** (get a notification before your shift starts), **Quick check-in / check-out notification** (a clock-in/out button right in the notification, phone),
  and — if you are a direct manager of employees — **Manager alerts**: **Employee is late** / **Employee leaves early** (notified when someone you manage
  checks in late or checks out before the shift ends, using the company's tolerance). Turn them on or off, then click **Save**.
- **Send test notification**: turn on at least one type and click it; a few seconds later the device that receives your alerts shows a notification (and it is also kept in the inbox). If you see nothing, check the app's notification
  permission in your phone settings; if the app says "Push notifications are not enabled on the server", tell your system administrator.
- The web version gets notifications too: the first time you click **Save** or **Send test notification** the browser asks for permission — choose **Allow**.
  On iPhone, add BossKin to the home screen and open it from there to receive them.

## Forgot your password

BossKin does not send password-reset emails. Tell your company's **administrator / HR**: they click **Reset password** next to your name
(key icon) on the **Employees** screen or **Roles → Users** and give you a temporary password. Sign in with it, then set a new password as described above.
