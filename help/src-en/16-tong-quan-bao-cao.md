## Dashboard

Open **Dashboard**. Filter by department in the top-right corner; pick a date with the *Today* button.

- **Overview** tab — new hires, resignations, attendance rate, latest period payroll, number present / on leave / absent, and today's attendance list. *Absent* counts only shifts that have **ended** without a check-in; shifts that have not started or are in progress are not counted.
- **Risks & Warnings** — contracts that need renewing, documents about to expire, and anomalies that need handling.
- **Attendance** tab — late arrivals, early departures and overtime by day.
- **Analytics** tab — monthly trend charts.

## Reports

Open **Reports**. Each report has an **Export to Excel** button; choose the period / department in the box that appears.

| Group | Reports |
|---|---|
| HR | Headcount statistics, new hires, resigned employees, insured employees, dependents |
| Year | Annual overview report |
| Attendance | Attendance summary, overtime summary |
| Payroll | Payroll cost summary (including employer insurance contributions) |

![Report list](img:reports)

> **Note:** Each report also requires permission to view the corresponding module (e.g. the payroll report requires permission to view payroll). People limited to a department scope
> must choose a department within their scope.
