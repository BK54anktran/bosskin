---

Open **Audit log**. Each row shows the action type, the object, the time and who did it. Click a row to see the **before → after** details.

![Audit log](img:audit)

Filter by **action type**, **performer's email** and **date range**.

What is recorded: editing / approving / deleting attendance, Excel attendance import, approving attendance requests, locking / unlocking attendance periods, every payroll action, changing roles /
permissions / scope, locking / unlocking accounts, resetting passwords, exporting temporary passwords, enabling / disabling / removing 2FA, removing devices, contracts, dependents,
documents (including every view), approving profile edits, job history, holidays, work calendars, and system administrators signing in on behalf of a company.

> **Note:** The log is read-only — nobody can edit or delete it.
