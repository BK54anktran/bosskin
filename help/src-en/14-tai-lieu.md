Open **HR documents**. Files are stored privately; each time you view / download one, the link works for only a few minutes and is recorded in the Audit log.

## Expiring

The latest copy of each type of paper that has an expiry (CCCD, certificates, health checkup certificates, etc.) will expire within the advance-notice number of days. Click **Download** to view it.

![Expiring documents](img:docs-expiring)

## Missing required

Employees who have no **approved, unexpired** copy of a document type marked *Required*.

![Employees missing required documents](img:docs-missing)

## Pending

Documents employees submit themselves from Personal info. Click ✓ to approve, ✕ to reject (enter a reason), ⬇ to view the file. You cannot approve your
own documents.

![Pending documents](img:docs-pending)

## Document types

The company's catalog (by default: CCCD, résumé, degrees, certificates, signed contract, health checkup certificate). For each type choose
**Required** (flags it as missing) and **Has expiry** (requires an expiry date).

![Document types](img:docs-types)

To upload a document for an employee: go to **Employees**, click 📁 on that person's row, then **Upload document** (PDF, image or Word, up to 10 MB;
it can be linked to that person's contract).
