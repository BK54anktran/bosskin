## See your remaining leave

Open **Leave** → **Current leave balance** tab. The balance is the total of your leave funds that are still valid.

![Leave balance](img:leave-balance)

## Create a leave request

1. Click **Create leave request**.
2. Choose the **Leave type** (annual leave, sick leave…).
3. Choose the **Request mode**: by day (one or several days) or by hour.
4. Choose **From date / To date** (or the time range) and enter a **Reason**.
5. Click **Create**.

![Create a leave request](img:leave-new)

BossKin counts the days automatically: it only counts **days you have a shift**, skipping holidays and days off per the calendar; hourly leave excludes mid-shift breaks.

> **Note:** For leave types that deduct from a fund, you need enough balance to create the request. Requesting leave on a holiday / a day without a shift will be rejected.

## Track and cancel requests

The **Leave requests** tab lists your requests with the status *Pending*, *Approved*, *Rejected*, *Cancelled*. If the company uses
2-step approval, a request also shows an *Awaiting manager* label until your direct manager approves.

A pending request has a **Cancel request** button. If an approved request is cancelled, the leave is returned to the same fund it was deducted from.
