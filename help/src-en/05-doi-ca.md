## Request a swap / give-away

1. Open **Shift swaps** → click **Request swap / give away**.
2. Choose **My shift** that you want to swap.
3. Choose a colleague and their shift (swap), or just choose the recipient (give away).
4. Enter a **Reason** and click **Send request**.

![List of shift swap requests](img:swaps)

![Request swap / give away dialog](img:swap-new)

The request must be accepted by a manager who has permission to approve shift swaps. After approval, the two people's shift assignments are swapped automatically.

> **Note:** You cannot swap a shift that already has attendance data or belongs to a locked period.

## Register your work schedule

When the company opens a registration round, the **Schedule registration** menu (*My space* group) shows the open round and its deadline.

1. For each day you want to work, turn on the switch and choose **Existing shift**, **Custom hours** (pick start – end time) or **Available all day** (the manager may assign any shift).
2. To work 2 slots in a day (e.g. morning and evening), click **Add 2nd slot**. At most 2 slots, and they must not overlap.
3. Click **Submit**. Until the round closes you can still edit and **Update** your registration; days the manager has approved stay unchanged.

Approved days get a shift assignment, which you see in **Check-in / Check-out** like a normal shift.
