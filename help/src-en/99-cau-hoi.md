## I can't see an item in the menu

The menu only shows items you have permission for. Ask your company administrator to check your role in **Roles**. Some items also depend on settings:
*Team approvals* only appears when two-step approval is on and you have direct reports. Entire feature groups (attendance, shifts, leave, payroll, contracts,
documents, reports) may also not be enabled for your company — contact the BossKin provider.

## I can't check in

See the error table in [My attendance](cham-cong-ca-nhan.html#check-in-check-out). The most common causes: location not allowed, standing outside the area,
a new phone (ask HR to remove the old device), or being outside the shift's time window. If you forgot to check in, send an **Attendance request**.

## My hours are wrongly counted as late / absent

Check your assigned shift and your department's tolerance. Send a time-correction request; once the manager approves, the attendance sheet updates. If the period is locked, ask HR to reopen
it.

## My payslip isn't showing

You only see a payslip after accounting has **approved** it. If it is approved but the figures are wrong, tell accounting before payday.

## My leave days are wrong

Open **Leaves → Current leave balance**; HR can check your *Deduction ledger* to see which balance each request deducted from. Leave is restored when a request is cancelled
or the manager rejects it.

## I forgot my password / lost the phone with my authenticator app

Tell your company administrator: **Reset password** (the key on the *Employees* screen or *Roles → User*) or **remove 2FA** in *Roles → User*. If you still have 2FA backup codes, use one to sign in.

## Switch to English

Click your avatar → **Language** → English.

## Contact support

If you hit an error, take a screenshot (including the **request ID** shown in the error message, if any) and send it to your company administrator or BossKin support.
