## Check-in / Check-out

Open **Check-in / Check-out** (*My space* group). The screen shows a map of your current location, today's shift and the allowed time window. The date card at the top has a **live clock in your company's time zone** (shown with the zone, e.g. `UTC+07:00 · Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh`) — punches are timed by this clock, not by your phone's.

1. Allow the browser / app **location access** when asked.
2. Check **Today's shifts** and the allowed *Check-in time / Check-out time* window.
3. Click **Check-in** when you start work; click **Check-out** at the end of the shift.

![Check-in / Check-out screen](img:checkin)

Cases where you cannot clock in:

| Error | Cause / what to do |
|---|---|
| Out of range | You are outside the allowed radius around the office / workshop. Move closer and try again. |
| Fake location | A fake-location app is on. Turn it off and try again. |
| Account bound to another device | The company enabled "clock in only on a registered device". Ask HR to remove the old device when you change phones. |
| Outside the time window | Too early / past the clock-in window of the shift. Send an **Attendance request** (section below). |
| You have no shift today | You have no shift on this day. If the company enabled "allow clock-in without an assigned shift", the screen says you can still clock in: clock in / out several times a day (finish one session before starting the next), each session is one attendance row, and hours are counted from actual times. |

> **Tip:** On a phone, turn on *Quick check-in / check-out notification* in Personal info to clock in straight from the reminder notification.

The **Attendance history** section at the bottom lists the days of the month with check-in / out times. Entries not yet approved by a manager show a **Pending** label.

## Send an attendance request (missed punch, time correction, overtime)

Use this when you forgot to clock in, clocked at the wrong time, or need to register overtime (OT).

1. Open **Attendance requests** → click **Create request**.
2. Choose the **date**; the dialog shows your shifts for that day.
3. Choose the **Request type** (*Check-in / Check-out* to correct times, or *Overtime*).
4. Enter the requested **check-in / check-out time** (click the clock icon to pick) and a note with the reason.
5. Click **Send request**.

![Create an attendance request](img:att-request-new)

The request stays in the **Pending** tab until a manager approves it. Once approved, the times in the timesheet change to match your request.

> **Note:** If the period is **locked**, you cannot send requests for days in it — contact HR.
