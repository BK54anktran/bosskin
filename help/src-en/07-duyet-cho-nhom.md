## When this appears

If the company chose **"Direct manager first, then HR"** (Company settings → Request approval) and you are the *direct manager* of at least
one person, the menu gains **Team approvals** with the number of requests waiting for you. You also get a notification when a report submits a request.

## Approve / reject

1. Open **Team approvals**. Requests are grouped by type: Leave, Attendance requests, Shift swaps.
2. Review the date, the number of days / hours and the reason.
3. Click **Approve** (the request moves to HR for the final step) or **Reject** (enter a reason; the request ends and the leave is refunded).

![Team approvals screen of a production supervisor](img:team-approvals)

> **Note:** HR cannot give the final approval until the manager has approved. If you are both the direct manager and have final-approval
> permission, a single click on Approve records both steps.

Who is whose direct manager is set by HR on the employee record (the *Direct manager* field) — see [Employees](nhan-vien.html).
